# District Layouts & Building List

The megacity is divided into 7 distinct zones:

## 1. Delhi Downtown (Modern Financial District)
- **Theme:** Glass towers, luxury, high-density corporate environment.
- **Key Buildings:**
  - DMC Tower (Delhi Municipal Corporation)
  - DMTA Central HQ
  - The Diamond Hotel (Luxury)
  - MegaMall Delhi (Convention Center & Shopping)

## 2. Purana Delhi (Old Heritage City)
- **Theme:** Historic, narrow alleys, dense markets, colonial architecture.
- **Key Buildings:**
  - The Grand Mosque
  - Red Stone Fort
  - Spice Market (Bazaars)
  - Colonial Post Office

## 3. Cyber Hub (Tech District)
- **Theme:** Futuristic, glass-and-steel, modern startup campuses.
- **Key Buildings:**
  - Nexus Data Center
  - Innovate IT Park
  - Cyber Plaza (AI Companies)

## 4. Gulmohar Enclave (Residential)
- **Theme:** Mixed residential, parks, schools, essential services.
- **Key Buildings:**
  - Sunrise Towers (High-rise apartments)
  - Gulmohar Villas (Independent gated houses)
  - Delhi City Hospital
  - Central Police Station

## 5. Delhi University Campus
- **Theme:** Open green spaces, academic buildings, sports facilities.
- **Key Buildings:**
  - Central Library (Dome structure)
  - Engineering Labs
  - Olympic-size Stadium
  - Student Hostels

## 6. Udyog Vihar (Industrial Area)
- **Theme:** Warehouses, logistics, heavy industry.
- **Key Buildings:**
  - Mega-Storage Facility
  - Delhi Power Substation
  - Freight Depot

## 7. Delhi International Airport (VIA)
- **Theme:** Massive modern aviation hub.
- **Key Buildings:**
  - Terminal 1 (Domestic)
  - Terminal 2 (International)
  - ATC Tower
  - Maintenance Hangars A & B
