# Delhi Metro Megacity: DMTA Network & Station Designs

The Delhi Mass Rapid Transit Authority (DMTA) is the lifeblood of the city. The network is vast, intricate, and fully integrated into the urban environment.

## 1. The 12-Line Blueprint
The network consists of 12 distinct lines, servicing ~85 stations across all districts.

* **Blue Line:** The main East-West artery. Connects the Cyber Hub, Downtown, and the Industrial Area. Mix of underground and elevated tracks.
* **Yellow Line:** The main North-South artery. Connects Purana Delhi (Heritage) down to Gulmohar Enclave (Residential). Mostly underground.
* **Red Line:** Historic commercial route running above ground on massive concrete viaducts through older city sections.
* **Green Line:** Suburban connector linking the University campus to the outer western edges.
* **Purple Line:** The Inner Circle line. Connects major interchanges without passing through the exact center.
* **Pink Line:** The Outer Ring route, primarily elevated, following the main ring road.
* **Orange Line (Airport Express):** High-speed, deep underground line. Very few stops. Connects DMTA Central HQ directly to VIA Terminals 1 & 2.
* **Silver Line:** Express line servicing the new tech parks in the Cyber Hub.
* **Aqua Line:** Scenic elevated route that follows the banks of the Neel River.
* **Brown Line:** Deep underground route servicing the dense Purana Delhi. Requires massive staircases/water elevators to reach.
* **Gold Line:** A short, luxurious underground loop servicing the high-end shopping districts in Downtown.
* **White Line:** Industrial worker transport linking the eastern suburbs directly to Udyog Vihar.

## 2. Standard Station Architecture
While aesthetic themes vary by district, all DMTA stations adhere to a strict layout to ensure realism.

* **Entrances:** Distinct glass or concrete street-level pavilions.
* **Concourse (Ticket Hall):**
  * Ticket vending machines (Custom player heads/dispensers).
  * Customer care booths (Villager NPCs).
  * Security checkpoints (End rods acting as metal detectors, string for luggage scanners).
  * Automated Fare Collection (AFC) gates (Iron trapdoors controlled by redstone/pressure plates).
* **Vertical Circulation:**
  * Escalators (Stair blocks with moving water beneath glass, or clever diagonal builds).
  * Elevators (Water columns for players, or complex redstone flying machines if performance allows).
* **Platform Level:**
  * Platform Screen Doors (PSDs) (Glass panes and iron doors that open automatically).
  * Tactile paving (Yellow concrete powder or yellow glazed terracotta along the edge).
  * Seating (Quartz/Iron stairs).
  * LED Passenger Information Displays (Glowing item frames or custom map art).
* **Back-of-House:** Electrical rooms, ventilation shafts (cobwebs/iron trapdoors), and staff break rooms.

## 3. Unique Station Concepts
* **DMTA Central (Interchange):** A massive 4-level underground cavern where the Blue, Yellow, and Orange lines intersect. Features a massive central skylight reaching up to the surface plaza.
* **Heritage Square (Brown Line):** Deep underground. Features arched red sandstone ceilings, intricate brickwork, and murals depicting city history.
* **Cyber City Elevated (Silver Line):** Sleek, aerodynamic white concrete structure with a continuous blue stained-glass canopy. Futuristic sea-lantern lighting.
* **Terminal 1 (Orange Line):** Integrated directly into the airport basement. Features massive concrete pillars, luggage carts, and direct elevator access to check-in.
* **Botanical Garden (Aqua/Pink Line):** At-grade station featuring living walls (leaves/vines) and an open-air concourse blending into the park.

## 4. Train Rolling Stock
Static train builds placed strategically along the lines and at platforms.

* **Standard Metro (6-8 Cars):** White concrete body with a colored stripe matching the line. Interior features iron trapdoor seating, redstone lamp lighting, and end-rod standing poles.
* **Airport Express:** More aerodynamic front (stairs/slabs). Plush interior with colored wool/carpet seats and designated luggage racks (scaffolding).
* **Maintenance Vehicle:** Flatbed cars carrying rails, gravel, and tools, pulled by a small yellow/black hazard-striped locomotive.

## 5. Signage & Wayfinding Templates
Clear navigation is crucial for a map of this scale.
* **Line Indicators:** Colored concrete or wool blocks placed alongside tracks to guide players if they are walking the tunnels.
* **Station Names:** Hanging signs (Mangrove for red text contrast, Birch for dark text) placed prominently on platforms.
* **Directional Banners:** Custom banners featuring arrows and the DMTA logo used in concourses.
* **System Maps:** Large walls utilizing custom Map Art to display the entire 12-line network in ticket halls.
