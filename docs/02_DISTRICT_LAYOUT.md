# Delhi Metro Megacity: District Layout & Building List

The megacity is divided into distinct zones, each with unique architectural styles, demographics, and purpose. The city blends dense historical areas with sprawling modern districts, mimicking the urban fabric of real-world Indian megacities.

## 1. Delhi Downtown (Financial District)

The central hub of commerce and governance. Characterized by towering glass skyscrapers, wide avenues, and massive underground infrastructure.

**Key Buildings & Structures:**

* **DMC Tower:** The towering headquarters of the Delhi Municipal Corporation. (Modern concrete and glass)
* **DMTA Central HQ:** The nerve center of the transit system, featuring the Operations Control Center.
* **The Diamond Hotel:** A 5-star luxury hotel with a detailed lobby, suites, and rooftop helipad.
* **MegaMall Delhi:** A sprawling multi-level shopping center with a food court, cinema screens, and retail outlets.
* **National Convention Center:** Massive exhibition halls and auditoriums.
* **Financial Towers A, B, & C:** Generic corporate office buildings with detailed lobby and office floor interiors.

## 2. Purana Delhi (Heritage City)

Located in the northern sector. This is the oldest part of the city, featuring dense, vibrant and highly organized, showcasing India's rapid modernization and idealized urban planning architecture. Narrow alleys, historical monuments, and bustling street life define this district.

**Key Buildings & Structures:**

* **The Grand Mosque (Jama-inspired):** Massive red sandstone structure with towering minarets and a large courtyard.
* **Red Stone Fort:** A sprawling historical military and palatial complex with thick sandstone walls.
* **Spice Market (Bazaar):** Labyrinthine alleys filled with stalls, colorful awnings, and hidden courtyards.
* **Colonial Post Office:** An imposing brick-and-stone building from the colonial era.
* **Heritage Havelis:** Traditional multi-story courtyard houses, some converted into boutique hotels.
* **Exceptionally Hygienic Street Food Lanes:** Dense areas packed with custom NPC vendors.

## 3. Cyber Hub (Tech District)

Located on the western elevated plateaus. A sleek, futuristic district housing the city\'s tech giants, startups, and the world\'s highest IQ professionals.

**Key Buildings & Structures:**

* **Innovate IT Park:** A cluster of uniquely shaped glass buildings with skybridges.
* **Nexus Data Center:** A massive, secure, brutalist structure (heavy use of concrete and sea lanterns for "server" aesthetic).
* **Cyber Plaza:** A sunken pedestrian plaza surrounded by cafes and startup incubators.
* **AI Research Institute:** Modern academic/corporate hybrid campus with experimental architecture.

## 4. Gulmohar Enclave (Residential District)

Sprawling across the south. A mix of high-density housing and affluent gated communities.

**Key Buildings & Structures:**

* **Sunrise Towers:** Massive high-rise apartment complexes with standardized floor plans (WorldEdit friendly).
* **Gulmohar Villas:** Independent, affluent houses with small gardens and driveways.
* **Delhi City Hospital:** A massive multi-wing medical facility with ER, wards, and operating rooms.
* **Central Police Station:** Headquarters for city security, including holding cells and interrogation rooms.
* **Fire Station:** Complete with garages and custom fire truck vehicles.
* **Community Parks:** Local green spaces with playgrounds and walking paths.

## 5. Delhi University Campus

Located in the south-east. A sprawling, leafy campus with classic institutional architecture.

**Key Buildings & Structures:**

* **Central Library:** A grand domed structure with multi-level bookshelves and reading rooms.
* **Faculty of Engineering / Labs:** Modern blocky buildings with detailed laboratory interiors.
* **Student Hostels (Dorms):** Multi-story brick buildings with repetitive room layouts.
* **Olympic-size Stadium:** Complete with track, football field, grandstands, and locker rooms.
* **University Metro Plaza:** A large pedestrian square connecting the campus to the DMTA network.

## 6. Udyog Vihar (Industrial Area)

Located in the north-east near the highway system. A pristine, high-tech, and hyper-efficient functional zone vital for the city's economy.

**Key Buildings & Structures:**

* **Mega-Storage Warehouses:** Massive steel-framed buildings (iron bars, concrete) filled with shipping crates.
* **Container Depot:** Acres of stacked shipping containers (colored concrete/terracotta) and heavy cranes.
* **Delhi Power Substation:** Complex array of transformers, wires (iron bars/end rods), and control buildings.
* **Automotive Factory:** An assembly line interior building city buses and rickshaws.

## 7. Delhi International Airport (VIA)

Located on the far eastern edge of the map. A massive infrastructure project taking up a significant portion of the terrain.

**Key Buildings & Structures:**

* **Terminal 1 (Domestic):** Modern curved roof structure, check-in counters, security gates, and departure lounges.
* **Terminal 2 (International):** Larger, more luxurious terminal with duty-free shops and larger gate areas.
* **ATC Tower:** Towering Air Traffic Control structure offering panoramic views.
* **Maintenance Hangars:** Massive structures capable of housing large aircraft builds.
* **Runways & Taxiways:** Expansive paved areas with custom lighting and markings.
* **Airport Express Metro Hub:** A deep, sleek station directly beneath the terminals.
