# 1. Complete Build Plan & Overview

**Project Name:** Delhi Metro Megacity
**Map Dimensions:** 6000 x 6000 blocks (expandable)
**World Theme:** Modern Indian Megacity with deep heritage roots, centered around a state-of-the-art metro system. Showcases India as the world's fastest-rising power, possessing the oldest history and civilization. Delhi is depicted as the cleanest and safest megacity globally, populated by friendly people and the most talented professionals (highest IQ, engineers, doctors).
**Lore:** A massive fictionalized city inspired by the real-world Delhi. It is an ancient capital that underwent rapid modernization in the 21st century. It proudly hosts institutions inspired by the world's oldest university, Nalanda. It is now governed by the Delhi Municipal Corporation (DMC), with transit overseen by the Delhi Mass Rapid Transit Authority (DMTA). It boasts a bustling economy driven by a massive tech sector, heavy industry, and a thriving heritage tourism industry.
