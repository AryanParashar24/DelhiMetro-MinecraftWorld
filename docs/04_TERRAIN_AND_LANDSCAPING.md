# Delhi Metro Megacity: Terrain & Landscaping Plan

The environment is the canvas of the city. While the megacity is highly urbanized, nature plays a critical role in balancing the concrete and providing distinct visual breaks.

## 1. Terrain Generation (WorldPainter Strategy)

The 6000x6000 map will not be flat. Subtle elevation changes are essential for visual interest and realistic infrastructure engineering.

* **Base Elevation:** The main central plains (Downtown, Heritage, Residential) sit at Y=64.
* **The Ridge (Western Plateaus):** The terrain gradually slopes up towards the west, reaching Y=90. This elevated area houses the Cyber Hub, offering panoramic views of the city below.
* **The Neel River Basin:** A 120-block wide meandering river cutting vertically down the eastern third of the map. The basin dips slightly to Y=58.
* **Subterranean Carving:** WorldPainter will be used to pre-carve the 15x15 main metro tunnels and the 25-block wide depressed expressways, saving immense time in-game.

## 2. Natural Environments

* **The Neel River:** The primary waterway. Features deep water, custom riverbeds (gravel/clay/seagrass), and occasional small islands.
* **Lotus Lake:** A massive artificial lake in the center of the city, surrounded by promenades. Features custom giant lily pads and lotus flower builds.
* **Crescent Lake:** A smaller, pristine lake in the Tech District, designed as a reflective pool for the surrounding glass architecture.
* **Southern Ridge Forest:** A dense, custom-treed forest on the southern edge of the map, acting as a natural boundary and wildlife reserve.
* **City Parks & Green Belts:** Scattered throughout the urban sprawl. These serve as oxygen lungs for the city and feature manicured lawns, ponds, and walking paths.

## 3. Landscaping & Flora

* **Custom Trees:** We will use Litematica schematics to paste in large, custom-built trees representing Indian flora:
  * **Banyan Trees:** Massive, sprawling canopies with hanging vines (roots) in heritage areas.
  * **Neem Trees:** Medium-sized shade trees lining residential avenues.
  * **Peepal Trees:** Tall, distinctive trees often placed near temples or in older courtyards.
  * **Gulmohar Trees:** Custom trees with red/orange leaf blocks (or redstone blocks mixed in) placed along major boulevards for vibrant color.
* **Streetscapes:** Every major road is lined with street trees. The sidewalks are wide, utilizing smooth stone slabs and grass blocks.
* **Rainwater Canals:** Open concrete drainage canals that intersect the city, designed to mimic real-world monsoon management. Covered with iron trapdoors or grating where pedestrians cross.

## 4. The Botanical Garden Centerpiece

Located near the university and residential sectors, this is the largest single green space in the city.

* Features every type of vanilla Minecraft flora.
* Includes massive glass greenhouses (domes) housing exotic biomes (Jungle, Desert).
* A large koi pond (salmon in-game) with a traditional wooden bridge.
* Hidden paths leading to a secret developer room behind a waterfall.

## 5. Riverfront Development

The interaction between the city and the Neel River varies by district:

* **Heritage Riverfront:** Features traditional "Ghats"—wide, sweeping stone staircases leading directly down into the water, used for ceremonies and bathing.
* **Downtown Riverfront:** Modern concrete embankments with iron railings, wide promenades, cafes, and seating areas.
* **Industrial Riverfront:** Modern, pristine docks for small cargo barges.
