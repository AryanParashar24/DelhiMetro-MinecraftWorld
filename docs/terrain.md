# Terrain & Environment Plan

## Overall Dimension & Biomes
- **Map Dimensions:** 6000 x 6000 blocks.
- **Base Biomes:** Plains/Savanna for the main city. River biome for the Neel River. Forest/Jungle for the southern border.

## WorldPainter Strategy
1. **Base Generation:** Flat plains at Y=64, rising gradually to Y=90 towards the southwest for the Tech District.
2. **Water Bodies:**
   - **The Neel River:** 120-block wide meandering river cutting through the eastern third.
   - **Artificial Lakes:** Lotus Lake in the center, Crescent Lake in the Tech District.
3. **Forests & Greenery:** Custom tree layers for Banyan, Neem, and Peepal trees on the outskirts.
4. **Transit Tunnels:** Pre-carved 15x15 tunnels for underground metros and large depressed highways.

## Landscaping & Natural Environment
- **Botanical Garden:** Centerpiece park featuring diverse flora, greenhouses, and a koi pond.
- **Streetscapes:** Major roads lined with custom street trees and wide, smooth stone pedestrian sidewalks.
- **Riverfront:** Traditional steps (Ghats) in the Heritage district, modern concrete embankments in the downtown.
- **Wildlife Reserves:** Forest patches on the edges of the city.
