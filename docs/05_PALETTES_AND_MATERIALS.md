# Delhi Metro Megacity: Block & Material Palettes

To maintain visual cohesion across the massive 6000x6000 world, strict material palettes are assigned to different architectural styles and districts. This prevents visual clutter and ensures the city feels unified yet diverse.

## 1. Modern Architecture (Downtown & Cyber Hub)

Focuses on sleek lines, glass facades, and clean concrete surfaces.

* **Primary Structural:** White Concrete, Light Gray Concrete, Cyan Terracotta, Cyan Concrete.
* **Glazing (Glass):** Cyan Stained Glass, Light Blue Stained Glass, Black Stained Glass, Tinted Glass (for high-contrast futuristic looks).
* **Accents:** Smooth Quartz, Iron Blocks, Sea Lanterns (for sleek lighting).
* **Flooring/Plazas:** Polished Andesite, Polished Diorite, Cyan Terracotta patterns.

## 2. Heritage Architecture (Purana Delhi)

Focuses on historical Indian aesthetics, heavily utilizing red tones, intricate carvings, and warm lighting.

* **Primary Structural:** Red Sandstone (all variants), Cut Sandstone, Smooth Sandstone, Bricks.
* **Accents:** Gold Blocks (for domes/finials), Stripped Jungle Logs, Stripped Acacia Logs.
* **Flooring:** Terracotta (uncolored), Red Nether Bricks (used sparingly for deep red accents), Mud Bricks.
* **Details:** Sandstone Walls, Sandstone Stairs/Slabs (for intricate archways and corbels).

## 3. Residential & Institutional (Gulmohar Enclave & University)

A blend of functional modernism and comfortable, grounded aesthetics.

* **Primary Structural (Apartments):** White Concrete, Gray Concrete, Bricks.
* **Primary Structural (University):** Bricks, Stone Bricks.
* **Accents:** Oak Planks, Spruce Planks, Glass Panes.
* **Flooring:** Polished Granite, Oak Planks, Stone.
* **Landscaping:** Azalea Leaves, Oak Leaves, Podzol (for gardens).

## 4. Industrial & Infrastructure (Udyog Vihar & Highways)

Focuses on pristine advanced engineering, cutting-edge automated machinery, and state-of-the-art structural integrity.

* **Primary Structural:** Stone Bricks, Cracked Stone Bricks, Gray Concrete, Yellow Terracotta (for hazard stripes).
* **Metalwork/Fencing:** Iron Bars, Anvils, Hoppers, Cauldrons.
* **Roads/Asphalt:** Deepslate (smooth and cobbled), Black Concrete Powder, Cyan Terracotta (for marked lanes).
* **Accents:** Redstone Lamps, End Rods (fluorescent tubes).

## 5. DMTA Metro Systems

The transit network has a distinct, clinical, and highly readable palette.

* **Station Concourse/Floors:** Smooth Stone, Polished Andesite.
* **Tactile Paving:** Yellow Concrete Powder, Yellow Glazed Terracotta (placed along platform edges).
* **Walls/Ceilings:** White Concrete, Quartz Pillars, Iron Trapdoors (for ventilation aesthetic).
* **Tracks:** Powered Rails, Detector Rails, Gravel (ballast), Smooth Stone Slabs.
* **Signage:** Glowing Item Frames, Mangrove/Crimson Signs (high contrast).

## 6. Lighting Fixtures

Lighting is crucial for preventing mob spawns while maintaining atmosphere.

* **Modern Interiors:** Sea Lanterns hidden under carpets, Froglights, End Rods (vertical or horizontal).
* **Heritage Exteriors:** Lanterns, Soul Lanterns, Campfires (hidden in chimneys).
* **Streetlights:** Deepslate Walls acting as poles, topped with Iron Bars, a Daylight Detector, and a Redstone Lamp.

## 7. Vehicles

* **Metro Trains:** White Concrete, specific Colored Concrete (matching the line), Iron Trapdoors, Glass Panes.
* **Auto-Rickshaws (Tuk-tuks):** Yellow/Green Concrete, Blackstone Stairs (wheels).
* **Buses:** Green/Red Concrete, Tinted Glass.
