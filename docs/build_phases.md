# Construction Phases & Build Plan

## Phase 1: Foundation & Transit (Weeks 1-4)
- **WorldPainter Generation:** Establish the base 6000x6000 terrain, carving out the Neel River and lakes.
- **Excavation & Tunneling:** Pre-carve the grid of 15x15 tunnels for the underground metro lines and 25-block wide depressed highways. Construct all tracks.
- **Structural Stations:** Build the core structural elements of major metro stations, including interchanges.

## Phase 2: Roadways & Infrastructure (Weeks 5-6)
- **Expressways & Highways:** Lay down major arterial roads, expressways, and ring roads.
- **Bridges & Flyovers:** Construct bridges over the Neel River and flyovers across the city.
- **Public Transit Corridors:** Establish Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) lanes and bus depots.

## Phase 3: District Shells (Weeks 7-10)
- **Financial District:** Erect the exteriors of massive glass skyscrapers and corporate towers.
- **Heritage District:** Construct the structural shells of narrow alleys, historic monuments, and colonial-era buildings.
- **Residential Zones:** Build high-rise apartment shells and independent housing layouts.
- **Industrial & Tech Zones:** Establish factories, warehouses, data centers, and IT parks.

## Phase 4: Detailing & Interiors (Weeks 11-15)
- **Complete Interiors:** Every important building must have a complete interior. This includes offices, apartments, shopping malls, hospitals, and police stations.
- **Station Detailing:** Fully detail metro stations with ticket halls, platform screen doors, staff rooms, and signage.

## Phase 5: Environment & Landscaping (Weeks 16-17)
- **Parks & Greenery:** Develop large parks, a botanical garden, and tree-lined boulevards.
- **Waterfronts:** Refine the riverfronts with modern embankments and traditional steps.
- **Street-Level Details:** Add streetlights, traffic signals, and pedestrian-friendly sidewalks.

## Phase 6: Gameplay & Optimization (Weeks 18-20)
- **Redstone & Mechanics:** Implement redstone-powered automatic train arrivals and functioning escalators/elevators.
- **NPCs & Loot:** Distribute custom NPCs, set up loot tables, and hide secrets.
- **Performance:** Optimize chunk loading, limit entities, and bake lighting.
