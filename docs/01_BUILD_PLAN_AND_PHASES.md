# Delhi Metro Megacity: Build Plan and Construction Phases

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Delhi Metro Megacity
**Map Dimensions:** 6000 x 6000 blocks (expandable)
**World Theme:** Modern Indian Megacity with deep heritage roots, centered around a state-of-the-art metro system.
**Transit Authority:** Delhi Mass Rapid Transit Authority (DMTA)

## 2. Complete Build Plan

The megacity will be developed with a focus on realism, aesthetics, and performance. The architecture will combine modern skyscrapers, massive infrastructure projects, and historical heritage sites. Every block must feel intentional, resulting in an immersive AAA-quality Minecraft experience.

### Core Objectives

* **Scale:** A massive 6000x6000 world with distinct districts.
* **Connectivity:** A fully integrated 12-line metro system (DMTA) covering all key areas.
* **Immersion:** Detailed interiors for all major buildings, secrets, and an alive atmosphere.
* **Playability:** Optimized for exploration, survival, adventure, and multiplayer roleplay.

## 3. Construction Phases

Building a map of this scale requires a structured approach to ensure consistency and prevent burnout. The project is divided into six major phases.

### Phase 1: Foundation & Transit (Weeks 1-4)

* **Terrain Generation:** Use WorldPainter to generate the 6000x6000 custom terrain (plains, river, lakes, elevated plateaus).
* **Metro Infrastructure:**
  * Pre-carve underground tunnels using WorldPainter and WorldEdit.
  * Lay down tracks for all 12 DMTA metro lines.
  * Construct the structural shells of all 70-100 metro stations (underground, elevated, at-grade).
* **Major Roads:** Layout the primary grid, including the main ring roads and expressways.

### Phase 2: Roadways & Infrastructure (Weeks 5-6)

* **Surface Transit:** Build the Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) corridors, bus depots, and parking lots.
* **Elevated Infrastructure:** Construct flyovers, pedestrian bridges, and elevated metro viaducts.
* **Waterways:** Detail the Neel River embankments, construct major bridges, and excavate rainwater canals.

### Phase 3: District Shells (Weeks 7-10)

* **Zoning:** Demarcate all 7 major districts (Financial, Heritage, Tech, Residential, University, Industrial, Airport).
* **Exterior Construction:** Build the outer shells of all major structures:
  * Glass skyscrapers in the Financial and Tech districts.
  * Sandstone monuments and dense housing in the Heritage district.
  * High-rise apartments and villas in the Residential zones.
  * Warehouses and factories in the Industrial area.
  * The two major terminals at the Airport.

### Phase 4: Detailing & Interiors (Weeks 11-15)

* **Building Interiors:** Fully furnish offices, apartments, shopping malls, hospitals, schools, and police stations.
* **Station Detailing:** Add ticket halls, escalators, security areas, platform screen doors, seating, and signage to all DMTA stations.
* **Transport Vehicles:** Build stationary standard metro trains, airport express trains, and maintenance vehicles on the tracks. Add electric buses and auto-rickshaws to the streets.

### Phase 5: Environment & Landscaping (Weeks 16-17)

* **Green Spaces:** Build large parks, the botanical garden, and forest patches.
* **Streetscapes:** Line avenues with custom trees, install streetlights, traffic signals, and finalize sidewalks.
* **Water Features:** Polish the artificial lakes and riverfront promenades.

### Phase 6: Gameplay, Lore & Optimization (Weeks 18-20)

* **Interactive Elements:** Implement redstone mechanisms (e.g., automated ticket gates, lighting).
* **Secrets & Lore:** Hide maintenance tunnels, the underground metro museum, developer rooms, and parkour sections. Place hidden loot.
* **Entity Placement:** Position custom NPCs (ticket vendors, corporate workers, street food vendors).
* **Performance Pass:** Optimize redstone, cull unnecessary entities, bake lighting, and perform lag checks.
